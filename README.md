## 📌 Project Overview

**KALORIQ** is a user-centered UI/UX design project focused on developing a digital platform for calorie monitoring and healthy lifestyle management.

The application was designed to address common difficulties users face when managing their daily nutrition and physical activity, such as monitoring calorie intake, understanding nutritional information, planning meals, maintaining exercise habits, and finding suitable food recommendations.

Rather than focusing only on calorie calculation, KALORIQ integrates multiple health and lifestyle features into a single digital experience.

The project applies a **User-Centered Design (UCD)** approach, beginning with user research and continuing through user needs analysis, persona development, storyboard creation, contextual modeling, UX goal definition, and interactive interface prototyping.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of KALORIQ are:

- Designing an intuitive digital platform for calorie and nutrition monitoring.
- Helping users understand and manage their daily nutritional intake.
- Supporting users in maintaining healthier lifestyle habits.
- Providing personalized food and meal recommendations.
- Integrating physical activity monitoring into the user experience.
- Providing health-related features through a single integrated application.
- Applying user-centered design principles to develop a user-oriented interface.
- Designing an interactive prototype that represents the proposed application experience.

## 🔎 UX Research

The design process was supported by quantitative user research conducted through a **Google Forms questionnaire**.

A total of **48 respondents** participated in the research.

### Research Approach

The questionnaire was used to understand users' habits, needs, expectations, and difficulties related to:

- Calorie monitoring
- Nutrition tracking
- Healthy eating
- Physical activity
- Meal planning
- Food recommendations
- Health and lifestyle management

The research targeted smartphone and internet users who have an interest in maintaining or improving a healthy lifestyle.

### Usability Aspects Considered

The research considered several usability dimensions:

- **Effectiveness** — whether users can accomplish their intended tasks.
- **Efficiency** — how easily and quickly users can complete tasks.
- **Safety** — whether the system supports users without creating unnecessary risks.
- **Utility** — whether the provided features are useful for users' needs.
- **Learnability** — how easily users can understand and learn the interface.
- **Memorability** — how easily users can remember how to use the application.
- **Satisfaction** — how satisfied users are with the overall experience.

## 👤 User Needs

Based on the research and design analysis, the application was designed around several user needs:

- Easy calorie calculation and monitoring.
- Simple nutrition tracking.
- Convenient meal planning.
- Personalized food recommendations.
- Information about nutritional content.
- Support for physical activity monitoring.
- Motivation to maintain healthy habits.
- Access to health-related guidance.
- An integrated platform instead of using multiple applications for different activities.
- A simple and understandable interface.

## ⚠️ User Pain Points

The design addresses several common pain points related to healthy lifestyle management:

- Difficulty consistently tracking daily calorie intake.
- Lack of convenient nutrition information.
- Difficulty planning balanced meals.
- Difficulty maintaining exercise motivation.
- Having to use different platforms for nutrition, exercise, and lifestyle tracking.
- Limited personalization in general health and calorie applications.
- Difficulty maintaining consistency in healthy habits.

## 🧑‍💻 User-Centered Design Process

The KALORIQ design process follows a user-centered approach consisting of several stages.

### 1. User Research

Questionnaires were distributed to collect information about users' behaviors, needs, preferences, and difficulties related to healthy lifestyle management.

### 2. User Persona

User personas were developed based on the research findings to represent the characteristics, goals, needs, and challenges of the target users.

### 3. User Needs Analysis

The research findings were translated into functional and experience requirements for the application.

### 4. Storyboard

Storyboards were used to illustrate how users could interact with KALORIQ in realistic daily-life situations.

### 5. Contextual Model

A contextual model was developed to describe the relationship between users, activities, information, and the application environment.

### 6. UX Goals

UX goals were established to guide the design toward an experience that is:

- Easy to understand
- Easy to navigate
- Informative
- Personalized
- Interactive
- Motivating
- Convenient for daily use

### 7. UI/UX Prototyping

The design concepts were transformed into an interactive high-fidelity prototype using Figma.

## ✨ Main Features

KALORIQ combines nutrition monitoring, physical activity, lifestyle management, and gamification into one application.

### 🏠 Home

The main dashboard provides users with an overview of their health and lifestyle activities.

Users can access important information and application features from a centralized interface.

### 🏆 RewardQ

A reward system designed to encourage users to maintain healthy habits.

Users can collect points and access available rewards or vouchers.

### 🏃 ActiQ

A physical activity monitoring feature designed to help users track and maintain their daily activity.

### 👤 Account

Provides users with access to their personal information, preferences, and account-related settings.

### 📚 QuideQ

Provides health and lifestyle-related guidance to support users in making healthier daily decisions.

### 🎯 ChallengeQ

A gamification feature that provides challenges to encourage users to stay active and maintain healthy habits.

### 🥗 NutriQ

A nutrition-focused feature that helps users monitor and understand their nutritional intake.

### 🍱 DietQ

Supports users in managing their diet and meal planning according to their lifestyle goals.

### 🔍 CheckQ

Provides a checking and monitoring experience related to users' health and lifestyle activities.

### 🍽️ RestoQ

Helps users discover food or restaurant options that can support their dietary and nutritional preferences.

### 🧮 Calorie Calculator

Allows users to calculate and monitor their estimated calorie requirements.

### 📊 Nutrition Tracking

Users can record and monitor nutritional intake to better understand their daily consumption.

### 🍴 Meal Plan

Provides meal planning functionality to help users organize their daily food consumption.

### 🔎 Food Analysis

Helps users analyze food and nutritional information.

### 💡 Food Recommendations

Provides food recommendations based on users' nutritional and lifestyle needs.

### 👩‍⚕️ Nutritionist & Diet Consultation

The concept includes access to professional nutrition and diet consultation to provide users with additional support.

### ⌚ Smartwatch Integration

The application concept supports integration with smartwatch devices for activity and health-related data monitoring.

### 🎮 Gamification

Gamification elements such as:

- Points
- Challenges
- Rewards
- Vouchers
- Achievement-oriented activities

are incorporated to encourage continued user engagement.
